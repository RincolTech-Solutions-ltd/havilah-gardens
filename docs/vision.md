# Havilah Gardens: vision and first scope

Status: draft, 10 Nov 2026. Esther and Hillary refine it as ideas come in.

## What Havilah Gardens is

A small business that makes and sells **planters, pots and flowers**. Esther leads the products: the designs, the
plants, the finish and how they are presented. The website is the shop window. It must make people want the
products and make it easy to buy them.

## Who buys

To be confirmed with Esther. Likely groups:

- people furnishing a home or balcony who want plants that look good straight away;
- people buying a gift;
- offices, cafés and small businesses that want plants and planters on site.

## What the website must do

Phase 1, market and take orders:

1. Show the products with good photos, names, sizes and prices.
2. Let a visitor ask about or order a product in one tap, through WhatsApp (ADR-003).
3. Tell the Havilah story: who makes the products and why.
4. Be quick on a cheap phone and easy to find on Google and social media.

Phase 2, sell online (only once phase 1 shows real demand):

5. A basket and online payment (mobile money and card).
6. Delivery areas and fees.
7. Order tracking for Esther: what is ordered, paid, made and delivered.

## What we do not know yet

These are open decisions, tracked in Hillary's project notes:

- Which market comes first, and so which currency, payment methods and delivery areas.
- The domain name.
- The first product list, with photos and selling prices (from Esther).
- Brand basics: logo, colours, tone of voice.
- Who answers the WhatsApp orders, and on which number.

## How we work

Ideas come from Esther through the Havilah Group WhatsApp chat or straight to Hillary. Each one becomes an issue
(see `docs/ideas/README.md`). Design and architecture decisions are written as ADRs in `docs/adr/` and the C4 model
in `docs/workspace.dsl` is kept current.
